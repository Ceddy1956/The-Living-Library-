# The-Living-Library-
Local-first literary cottage and botanical reading sanctuary. Organize custom wooden bookshelves, import .TXT/.PDF/.EPUB books, read in swipe or vertical scroll modes, tend a mossy Zen garden with Capkin's mushroom shop, decorate shelves, and listen to a 5-song Web Audio lofi gramophone alongside Barnaby the cat.   

## Overview

**The Living Library** transforms digital reading into a cozy cottage retreat. Books are shelved on customizable wooden bookcases, pages turn into Dewdrops that nurture a living mossy garden, and an interactive black cat named Barnaby patrols the sanctuary while a built-in Web Audio synthesizer plays lofi beats and rain ambiance.

Everything runs directly in your browser with no accounts, no cloud uploads, and no build tools required.

---

## Features

### Wooden Bookshelves & Shelf Decorator
* **Multi-Format Book Importer:** Import `.txt`, `.pdf` (via `PDF.js`), and `.epub` (via `JSZip`) volumes, or create and edit custom books with bespoke cover art and spine colors.
* **Pre-Loaded Classics & Reflections:** Ships with *Tuesdays with Morrie*, *Ego Is the Enemy*, *A Gentle Reminder*, and *Atomic Habits* ready to read.
* **Dynamic Shelf Grouping & Filtering:** Organize shelves by Genre, Reading Preference, Cover Color, or Author, and filter by search query or favorites.
* **Interactive Bookshelf Decorator:** Customize shelf wood/paint finishes (Classic Walnut, Honey Oak, Sage Paint, Teapot Teal, and more) and place 23 unlockable decorations—such as hanging ivy, fairy lights, ceramic cats, terrariums, and brass lanterns—onto specific shelf zones or drag them freely.
* **Portable `.JSON` Backups:** Export and restore your entire library, garden state, streaks, and shelf decorations as a `.json` archive.

 Interactive Reading Room
Dual Reading Modes:** Switch between horizontal swipe/drag page-flip mode (with keyboard arrow support) and continuous vertical scroll mode.
Typography & Paper Themes:** Choose from 7 classic serif and monospace fonts, adjust font size (`14px`–`26px`), and switch between White, Sepia, and Charcoal Black page themes (plus global Night Lamp mode).
Marginalia, Bookmarks & Highlights:** Highlight passages directly on the page, place silk page bookmarks, rate books, and record personal notes.

Living Moss Zen Garden & Capkin's Shop Stall
Free-Form Botanical Planting:** Earn Dewdrops by turning pages and logging reading sessions, then plant 27 types of vegetables and flowers directly onto a wind-reactive mossy lawn.
Dynamic Sanctuary Weather:** Switch between Clear Breeze, Rainstorm, Autumn Leaves, and Winter Snow with live canvas particle effects and wind sway physics.
Capkin's Lemonade & Botanical Stall:** Trade Dewdrops and harvested crops with Capkin the orange mushroom shopkeeper for Honey Citrus Lemonade, Golden Spore Fertilizer, Gourmet Salmon Feasts, Mystery Heirloom Seeds, shelf decorations, and shelf finishes.

# Streaks, Journal & Cozy Companions
28-Day Streak Calendar & Reading Journal:** Tracks daily reading minutes against your custom goal, updates streaks automatically, and stores personal journal entries.
Barnaby — Black Cat Assistant:** An animated vector cat that roams the bottom of the screen with interactive poses (Walk, Sit Tall, Arch & Jump, Eat Salmon, and Sleep) and sound cues.
5-Song Lofi Gramophone:** A procedural Web Audio API music engine featuring 5 original chill chord progressions, Rhodes-style tones, lo-fi percussion, and toggleable rain/vinyl crackle ambiance.
Distraction-Free Sanctuary Mode:** Press `H` anytime to hide the UI with a wooden flute chime and enjoy the ambient scene.
